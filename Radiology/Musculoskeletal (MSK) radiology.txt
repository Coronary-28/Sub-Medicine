Which of the following is FALSE regarding this lesion? [img: nonossifyingfibroma.png ]
A) Eccentrically located in the metaphysis
B) Size greater than 3 cm
C) Associated with periosteal reaction
D) Well-defined lesion with a sclerotic rim
Correct Answer: C) Associated with periosteal reaction
Explanation: A fibrous cortical defect/non-ossifying fibroma shows No periosteal reaction, cortical breach, or soft tissue mass — its benign nature is defined by an eccentric, sclerotic-rimmed lucency without aggressive features.

What is the name of this bone? [img: navicularanatomy.png ]
A) Navicular
B) Cuboid
C) Talus
D) Cuneiform
Correct Answer: A) Navicular
Explanation: The navicular is the medially positioned tarsal bone articulating proximally with the talar head and distally with the cuneiforms, distinguishing it from the more laterally placed cuboid and the proximal talus.

Which stage of Salter-Harris fracture is shown? [img: salterharris3.png ]
A) Type 2
B) Type 4
C) Type 3
D) Type 5
Correct Answer: C) Type 3
Explanation: The fracture plane runs along the growth plate and extends down through the epiphysis into the joint while sparing the metaphysis — the defining pattern of a Salter-Harris type III injury, which carries a poorer prognosis due to epiphyseal/articular involvement.

What is the earliest sign of this condition? [img: earliestsignofsacroiliitis.png ]
A) Sacroiliitis
B) Sunburst
C) Dagger sign
D) Bamboo spine
Correct Answer: A) Sacroiliitis
Explanation: Sacroiliitis (initial widening followed by erosions and sclerosis of the SI joints) is the earliest manifestation of ankylosing spondylitis; the dagger and bamboo spine signs reflect late-stage spinal ligamentous ossification and fusion.

Which is NOT a feature of a simple bone cyst?
A) Well-demarcated
B) Benign
C) Peripherally located
D) Radiolucent
Correct Answer: C) Peripherally located
Explanation: Unicameral (simple) bone cysts are centrally located within the metaphysis of long bones, not peripheral; they are otherwise well-defined, radiolucent, benign lesions with a narrow zone of transition.

What is the most likely diagnosis? [img: osteochondroma.png ]
A) Osteochondroma
B) Osteosarcoma
C) Enchondroma
D) Bone cyst
Correct Answer: A) Osteochondroma
