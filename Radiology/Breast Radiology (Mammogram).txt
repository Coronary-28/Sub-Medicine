Probably benign breast lesion that requires short-interval follow-up is which BI-RADS category?
A) BI-RADS 0
B) BI-RADS 1
C) BI-RADS 3
D) BI-RADS 4A
E) BI-RADS 2
Correct Answer: C) BI-RADS 3
Explanation: BI-RADS 3 means probably benign (<2% risk of malignancy), and short-interval follow-up is suggested. BI-RADS 2 is definitely benign (0%) and needs no short-interval follow-up. BI-RADS 4A is already suspicious (2–9%) and biopsy is considered.

Which of the following is a feature of a benign axillary lymph node on mammography?
A) Central lucency (fatty hilum)
B) Large, rounded shape without a hilum
C) Increased density with cortical thickening
D) Irregular or indistinct margins
E) Calcifications within the node
Correct Answer: A) Central lucency (fatty hilum)
Explanation: A normal or benign node is small and bean-shaped with a fatty hilum, which appears as central lucency. Loss of the hilum, rounding, increased density, and irregular margins all suggest malignancy.

What is the best first imaging modality for a pregnant patient with a palpable breast lump?
A) Bilateral mammography (CC and MLO)
B) Contrast-enhanced breast MRI
C) CT of the chest
D) Ultrasound
E) Spot-compression magnification mammography
Correct Answer: D) Ultrasound
Explanation: Ultrasound uses no ionizing radiation and is the modality of choice for symptomatic pregnant or young (<30 years) patients. It also distinguishes cystic from solid lesions and can guide biopsy. Mammography and CT use radiation, and MRI needs gadolinium, so none of them is first-line.

Which mammographic calcification morphology carries the highest suspicion of malignancy?
A) Coarse popcorn-like
B) Rim (eggshell)
C) Amorphous
D) Fine pleomorphic
E) Fine linear / fine linear branching
Correct Answer: E) Fine linear / fine linear branching
Explanation: Thin, irregular, branching calcifications (<0.5 mm) suggest deposition within a duct and carry the highest suspicion. Fine pleomorphic and amorphous are also suspicious but less so. Popcorn-like (involuting fibroadenoma) and rim calcifications are typically benign.

Which of the following features suggests a malignant breast mass on imaging?
A) Round shape with circumscribed margins
B) Oval shape with posterior acoustic enhancement
C) Irregular shape with spiculated, indistinct, or obscured margins
D) Fat-containing mass
E) Coarse popcorn-like calcification within the mass
Correct Answer: C) Irregular shape with spiculated, indistinct, or obscured margins
Explanation: Irregular shape and spiculated or indistinct margins are the most suspicious mass features for malignancy. Round or oval circumscribed masses are usually benign (cyst or fibroadenoma). What is the main value of obtaining both CC and MLO views in mammography?
A) Reducing the radiation dose
B) Giving a three-dimensional localization of a lesion
C) Differentiating solid from cystic lesions
D) Assessing lesion vascularity
E) Replacing ultrasound in dense breasts
Correct Answer: B) Giving a three-dimensional localization of a lesion
Explanation: The CC view shows whether a lesion is inner or outer, and the MLO view shows whether it is upper or lower. Together they localize it in three dimensions. Differentiating solid from cystic is the role of ultrasound.
