A 31-year-old woman claims sexual assault 4 days ago. Examination shows a recent hymenal tear at the 5 o'clock position. Which statement is TRUE?
A) No informed consent is required before examination
B) Delay in reporting confirms a fabricated story
C) Signs of injury corroborate and support the claim, though not confirmatory alone
D) Rupture of the hymen is an absolute sign confirming rape
E) The findings determine the exact time of the assault
Correct Answer: C) Signs of injury corroborate and support the claim, though not confirmatory alone
Explanation: A recent hymenal tear is corroborative evidence supporting the allegation, but it alone cannot confirm rape; consent is required before examination, and delayed reporting is common even in genuine cases.

Which of the following is NOT true concerning sexual assault victims?
A) Bestiality/paedophilia is a common practice among 30% of young adults
B) Post-assault forensic sampling is vital for evaluation
C) Victims should be assessed for genitourinary health, contraception needs, and counseling
D) Emergency contraception may include oral contraceptives
E) STI prophylaxis should be offered
Correct Answer: A) Bestiality/paedophilia is a common practice among 30% of young adults
Explanation: This statistic is false — paedophilia and bestiality are not common practices in any population; the other statements reflect appropriate sexual assault management.

Which of the following is a recognized type of hymen?
A) Notch at 3 o'clock
B) Notch at 9 o'clock
C) Elastic
D) Cribriform
Correct Answer: D) Cribriform
Explanation: Cribriform hymen (with multiple small openings) is a recognized anatomical variant; notches at 3 or 9 o'clock are considered normal artefacts rather than named types, and "elastic" is not a recognized hymen type.

What is the most common cause of erectile dysfunction (impotence) in young men?
A) Vascular disease
B) Psychogenic causes
C) Endocrine disease
D) Neurological disease
Correct Answer: B) Psychogenic causes
Explanation: In young men, erectile dysfunction is most commonly psychogenic in origin, whereas vascular causes become more predominant with advancing age.
