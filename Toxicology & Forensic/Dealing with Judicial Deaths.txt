Which of the following deaths is classified as a medico-legal case requiring investigation?
A) Death occurring while in prison/state custody
B) Death from a known natural disease at home
C) Hospital death from a previously diagnosed disease
D) Death from chronic illness under stable medical care
Correct Answer: A) Death occurring while in prison/state custody
Explanation: All deaths occurring in state custody must be investigated as medico-legal cases to rule out foul play and ensure accountability, unlike expected natural deaths under known medical care.

Which of the following can cause myocardial infarction WITHOUT underlying atheroma?
A) Uncontrolled hypertension
B) High lipids/cholesterol
C) Intense emotional stress causing coronary vasospasm
D) Uncontrolled diabetes mellitus
Correct Answer: C) Intense emotional stress causing coronary vasospasm
Explanation: Severe emotional stress can trigger Prinzmetal-type coronary vasospasm, producing MI in the absence of atherosclerotic plaque — a medico-legally significant distinction from atheroma-driven causes like hypertension, dyslipidemia, or diabetes.
###
A person dies in prison from bronchospasm. What is the correct course of action?
A) The physician must legally report the death to the public prosecutor
B) A standard death certificate is sufficient
C) No autopsy is required
D) Family consent is needed before autopsy
Correct Answer: A) The physician must legally report the death to the public prosecutor
Explanation: Deaths in state custody are mandatory medico-legal cases; the physician is legally obligated to notify the public prosecutor regardless of the apparent cause of death.

Which of the following causes of death is NOT considered medico-legal?
A) Pulmonary embolism following hip fracture in hospital
B) Pneumonia in a 16-year-old in a juvenile detention facility
C) Brought-in-dead 30-year-old following head injury
D) Tuberculosis death in a 30-year-old refugee under active medical care
E) Sudden death of a 60-year-old while awaiting an X-ray
Correct Answer: D) Tuberculosis death in a 30-year-old refugee under active medical care
Explanation: Death from tuberculosis under active, established medical care represents an expected natural death; the other scenarios involve trauma, custodial settings, or unexplained sudden death, all of which qualify as medico-legal.

When a forensic physician is called to a scene by the public prosecutor, what are the PRIMARY purposes?
A) Only to confirm death
B) Identification of the deceased and investigation of the circumstances of death
C) Only to collect toxicology samples
D) To clean and document the scene
Correct Answer: B) Identification of the deceased and investigation of the circumstances of death
Explanation: The forensic physician's scene role centers on identifying the body and estimating the time, cause, and manner of death to help reconstruct events for the prosecutor, rather than the narrower or unrelated tasks listed.
###
Which stage of lobar pneumonia is most likely to cause death in children?
A) Stage of red hepatization
B) Stage of congestion (1st stage)
C) Stage of grey hepatization
D) Stage of resolution
Correct Answer: B) Stage of congestion (1st stage)
Explanation: The congestion stage causes the most severe toxemia and is the most dangerous phase of lobar pneumonia, particularly in children, compared to the later hepatization and resolution stages.

What is the most common cause of sudden unexpected death?
A) Respiratory disease
B) Central nervous system disease
C) Cardiovascular disease (CAD > MI > hypertension)
D) Metabolic disease
Correct Answer: C) Cardiovascular disease (CAD > MI > hypertension)
Explanation: Coronary artery disease, acute myocardial infarction, and hypertensive heart disease together account for the majority of sudden unexpected deaths in adults, outweighing respiratory, CNS, or metabolic causes.
