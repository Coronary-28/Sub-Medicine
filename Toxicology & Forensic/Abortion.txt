Under Jordanian law, abortion is permitted under which of the following circumstances?
A) When requested by the mother for personal reasons
B) When continuing the pregnancy poses a serious risk to the mother's life or health
C) For fetal anomaly alone
D) Up to 12 weeks of gestation for any reason
Correct Answer: B) When continuing the pregnancy poses a serious risk to the mother's life or health
Explanation: Jordanian law criminalizes abortion except when continuing the pregnancy would seriously endanger the mother's life or health; it is not permitted on demand, for socioeconomic reasons, or for fetal anomaly alone.
###
Abortion in Jordanian law is allowed:
A) On demand
B) For socioeconomic reasons
C) Up to 12 weeks for any reason
D) For fetal anomaly alone
E) To save the mother's life
Correct Answer: E) To save the mother's life
Explanation: Under the Jordan Penal Code, abortion is a criminal offense except when continuation of pregnancy poses a serious risk to the mother's life.
