Issuing the initial medical report in a medico-legal case is the responsibility of:
A) Medical Officer
B) Surgical Specialist
C) The first physician who manages the patient in the emergency department
D) Forensic Pathologist
Correct Answer: C) The first physician who manages the patient in the emergency department
Explanation: The primary medico-legal report is always issued by the first doctor to examine the patient, regardless of specialty, to ensure timely and accurate documentation.
###
Who gives the PRIMARY medical report:
A) The first surgeon who met the patient
B) The first doctor who met the patient
C) Senior or head of department
D) Forensic doctor
Correct Answer: B) The first doctor who met the patient
Explanation: Legal responsibility for the initial medico-legal report falls on the first physician to assess the patient, irrespective of specialty.
###
Reporting and documentation of injuries will include all of the following EXCEPT one:
A) Injury location (anatomical measure distance from landmark)
B) Type (e.g. bruise, laceration, abrasion)
C) Estimate age of wound
D) Assailant description whether left or right hand user
E) Pain and/or Tenderness
Correct Answer: D) Assailant description whether left or right hand user
Explanation: Medico-legal documentation records the victim's objective injury findings; assailant handedness is an investigative inference, not a clinical documentation item.
###
According to Jordanian Public Health Law, a physician MUST report which of the following to the Ministry of Health:
A) All cases of trauma
B) All deaths
C) Notifiable infectious diseases
D) Only sexually transmitted diseases
Correct Answer: C) Notifiable infectious diseases
Explanation: Jordanian public health law mandates reporting notifiable/communicable diseases (e.g., cholera, typhoid) to the Ministry of Health, not all trauma or deaths generally.
###
A 60-year-old man underwent arthroscopy and meniscectomy, and died of pulmonary embolism. Which post-op care was NEGLIGENT?
A) Not informed how serious it is if he feels headache
B) Not informed of the kind of painkillers to use
C) Not told to have residence within 2 miles of hospital
D) Not informed of how serious it is if he feels short of breath
E) Not told the duration of disablement from work
Correct Answer: D) Not informed of how serious it is if he feels short of breath
Explanation: Pulmonary embolism is a known post-surgical complication presenting with dyspnoea; failing to warn the patient about this warning sign constitutes negligence.
###
A surgeon under the effect of alcohol performed a laparotomy, resulting in the patient's death. The surgeon is guilty of:
A) Contributory medical negligence
B) Criminal medical negligence
C) Civil medical negligence
D) Therapeutic misadventure
E) Ethical malpraxis
Correct Answer: B) Criminal medical negligence
Explanation: Operating while impaired by alcohol is a reckless disregard for patient safety with full knowledge of the risk, rising to the level of criminal negligence rather than mere civil liability.
###
Components of a medico-legal report are:
A) Only findings and diagnosis
B) History and physical examination only
C) Laboratory results and imaging only
D) Heading (preamble) + Body (findings) + Conclusion (opinion)
Correct Answer: D) Heading (preamble) + Body (findings) + Conclusion (opinion)
Explanation: A complete medico-legal report consists of three parts: the preamble (who/what/when/where), the body of objective findings, and the conclusion containing professional opinion.
###
In Jordan, a physician is legally governed by:
A) Only the Medical Association Law
B) Only the Public Health Law
C) Medical Association Law + Public Health Law + Jordanian Penal Code
D) International medical ethics codes only
Correct Answer: C) Medical Association Law + Public Health Law + Jordanian Penal Code
Explanation: Jordanian physicians are bound by three combined legal frameworks: the Medical Association Law, the Public Health Law, and the Penal Code (governing criminal negligence).
