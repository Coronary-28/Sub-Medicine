Reporting and documentation of injuries include all of the following EXCEPT:
A) Injury location (anatomical measurement from a fixed landmark)
B) Type of injury (e.g., bruise, laceration, abrasion)
C) Description of the assailant (left- or right-handed)
D) Estimated age of the wound
Correct Answer: C) Description of the assailant (left- or right-handed)
Explanation: The medico-legal report documents the victim's injuries objectively (site, type, size, age, tenderness). Assailant handedness is an investigative inference, not a clinical finding.
###
According to Jordanian law, a physician is obliged to report which of the following to the Ministry of Health?
A) Cases of trauma
B) Notifiable infectious diseases
C) All deaths
D) Sexually transmitted diseases only
Correct Answer: B) Notifiable infectious diseases
Explanation: The Public Health Law obliges the physician to notify the Ministry of Health of notifiable infectious diseases (e.g., cholera). It does not extend to all trauma cases or all deaths, nor is it limited to sexually transmitted diseases.
###
Who is responsible for issuing the primary medico-legal report?
A) The forensic physician
B) The senior physician or head of department
C) The first physician who examines or manages the patient, regardless of specialty
D) The surgeon on duty
Correct Answer: C) The first physician who examines or manages the patient, regardless of specialty
Explanation: The primary report is written by the first doctor who sees the patient, usually in the emergency department, whatever his specialty. The forensic physician completes the final report later.
###
The components of a medico-legal report are:
A) Findings and diagnosis only
B) Heading (preamble), body (history and findings), and conclusion (opinion)
C) History and physical examination only
D) Laboratory and imaging results only
Correct Answer: B) Heading (preamble), body (history and findings), and conclusion (opinion)
Explanation: A complete report has three parts: the heading identifying the case and the requesting authority, the body containing the history and examination findings, and the conclusion giving the medical opinion.
###
A surgeon operated while under the influence of alcohol, and the patient died. The surgeon is guilty of:
A) Contributory medical negligence
B) Civil medical negligence
C) Therapeutic misadventure
D) Ethical malpractice
E) Criminal medical negligence
Correct Answer: E) Criminal medical negligence
Explanation: Gross negligence or recklessness (operating while intoxicated) that causes death constitutes criminal negligence. Civil negligence results in compensation only, and therapeutic misadventure is an accident despite proper care.
###
In Jordan, a physician is legally governed by:
A) The Medical Association Law only
B) The Public Health Law only
C) International medical ethics codes only
D) The Medical Association Law, the Public Health Law, and the Jordanian Penal Code
Correct Answer: D) The Medical Association Law, the Public Health Law, and the Jordanian Penal Code
Explanation: The physician's practice is regulated by all three Jordanian laws together; international codes are not by themselves legally binding.
###
A 60-year-old man underwent arthroscopy with meniscectomy and died of pulmonary embolism. Which aspect of his post-operative care was negligent?
A) He was not informed how serious a headache would be
B) He was not informed which analgesics to use
C) He was not told to stay within 2 miles of the hospital
D) He was not informed of the duration of work disablement
E) He was not informed how serious shortness of breath would be
Correct Answer: E) He was not informed how serious shortness of breath would be
Explanation: After lower-limb surgery, shortness of breath is the warning sign of pulmonary embolism, and the patient must be told it requires urgent medical attention. The other omissions are not related to the cause of death.
