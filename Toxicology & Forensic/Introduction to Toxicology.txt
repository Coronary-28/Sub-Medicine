What type of toxicology concerns itself with medical and legal aspects, focused on detecting poisons in medicolegal investigations?
A) Clinical Toxicology
B) Environmental Toxicology
C) Forensic Toxicology
D) Analytical Toxicology
Correct Answer: C) Forensic Toxicology
Explanation: Forensic toxicology represents the intersection of toxicology with the law, applying poison detection specifically to medicolegal investigations, distinguishing it from clinical (patient management), environmental, and purely analytical toxicology.

What color is associated with methemoglobin?
A) Bright red
B) Cherry red
C) Bluish purple
D) Brownish
Correct Answer: D) Brownish
Explanation: Methemoglobin (Fe³⁺ state) produces a characteristic chocolate-brown discoloration of blood and livor, and causes cyanosis that fails to improve with supplemental oxygen — distinguishing it from cherry-red (carbon monoxide) or cyanotic bluish-purple (hypoxia) presentations.
