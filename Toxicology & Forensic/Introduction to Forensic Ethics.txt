Which of the following is the main ethical priority of the physician when dealing with vulnerable children who are victims of violence?
A) Protection of secrecy
B) Respect for confidentiality
C) Protection through early detection
D) Respect for the autonomy of the parents
E) Notification to the authorities
Correct Answer: C) Protection through early detection
Explanation: In suspected child abuse, the physician's primary ethical duty is early detection and protection of the child. This outranks confidentiality and parental autonomy.

In which of the following situations is breach of confidentiality permissible?
A) Reporting a notifiable infectious disease (e.g., cholera) to the health authorities
B) Request by the patient's husband or wife
C) Request by an insurance company representative
D) Reporting rheumatoid arthritis to the patient's employer
E) Request by a relative of the patient
Correct Answer: A) Reporting a notifiable infectious disease (e.g., cholera) to the health authorities
Explanation: Notifiable diseases must be reported to the Ministry of Health by law. Requests from a spouse, insurer, employer, or relative do not justify a breach without the patient's consent.
###
All of the following are principles of bioethics EXCEPT:
A) Autonomy
B) Justice
C) Non-maleficence
D) Reluctance
E) Beneficence
Correct Answer: D) Reluctance
Explanation: The four principles of bioethics are autonomy, beneficence, non-maleficence, and justice. "Reluctance" is not a bioethical principle.
###
Which of the following is NOT correct concerning valid consent prior to medical treatment?
A) It must be voluntary and freely given
B) Consent of the husband or wife is always mandatory
C) The patient must be informed of what the treatment involves
D) The patient must be informed of the benefits and risks
E) The capacity of the patient must be assessed
Correct Answer: B) Consent of the husband or wife is always mandatory
Explanation: Valid consent requires voluntariness, adequate information, and capacity. A competent adult consents for himself or herself, and requiring spousal consent would violate patient autonomy.
###
A 23-year-old single adult female presents with vaginal bleeding. Which of the following statements regarding consent is correct?
A) Consent must be obtained from her father or husband
B) Consent of her partner is always mandatory
C) A guardian must be present
D) A court order is required before treatment
E) She can give her own medical consent independently
Correct Answer: E) She can give her own medical consent independently
Explanation: Any competent adult (18 years or older) has full capacity to consent to treatment without parental, spousal, or guardian authorization.
###
What is the most important rule when performing a physical examination of the intimate parts of the body?
A) The examination must be performed only after police notification
B) The examination must be performed by a physician of the same gender only
C) A chaperone must be present
D) The examination must be photographed before it begins
E) The examination must be performed in the presence of a guardian
Correct Answer: C) A chaperone must be present
Explanation: A chaperone protects the dignity of the patient and the professional integrity of the physician during examination of intimate parts.
###
"Respect for persons" in the ethical approach includes all of the following EXCEPT:
A) Following the doctor's orders
B) Autonomy
C) Integrity
D) Equity
E) Equality
Correct Answer: A) Following the doctor's orders
Explanation: Respect for persons comprises autonomy, integrity, equity, and equality. Following the doctor's orders contradicts patient-centred ethics.
###
Which of the following is NOT true concerning the duties of doctors to patients?
A) Respect the patient's right to confidentiality
B) Always bear in mind the obligation to respect human life
C) Refer the patient to another physician whenever the examination is beyond the physician's capacity
D) Informed consent is not necessary when dealing with victims of violence
E) Act in the patient's best interest when providing medical care
Correct Answer: D) Informed consent is not necessary when dealing with victims of violence
Explanation: Informed consent is required for victims of violence as for any other patient. Only a true emergency in which delay would cause harm waives it.
###
Which of the following is NOT appropriate considering ethical standards?
A) Maintain professional competence throughout one's career
B) Enhance intimate relationships with patients
C) Deal honestly with patients
D) Enhance a multidisciplinary approach
E) Respect patient confidentiality
Correct Answer: B) Enhance intimate relationships with patients
Explanation: An intimate relationship between a physician and a patient is unethical because it violates professional boundaries.
###
Which of the following is NOT a declaration of the World Medical Association related to the humanization of medical practice?
A) Declaration of Geneva
B) Declaration of Helsinki
C) Declaration of Oslo
D) Declaration of Tokyo
E) Declaration of London
Correct Answer: E) Declaration of London
Explanation: The WMA declarations include Geneva, Helsinki, Oslo, Tokyo, and Sydney. There is no "Declaration of London" in medical ethics.
###
The humanization of medical practice was the main theme of which of the following?
A) Nuremberg Code
B) Oslo Declaration
C) Geneva Declaration
D) Tokyo Declaration
E) Ottawa Declaration
Correct Answer: C) Geneva Declaration
Explanation: The Declaration of Geneva (1948) is the physician's oath and defines the physician's duties. The Nuremberg Code (1947) concerns consent in research, Oslo concerns therapeutic abortion, and Tokyo concerns torture.
###
The Declaration of Helsinki is related to:
A) Humans in medical research
B) Violence
C) Child abuse
D) Torture of prisoners
E) Therapeutic abortion
Correct Answer: A) Humans in medical research
Explanation: The Declaration of Helsinki (1964) sets the ethical standards for medical research on human subjects. Torture is addressed by Tokyo and therapeutic abortion by Oslo.
###
All of the following are types of consent EXCEPT:
A) Implied consent for medical examination
B) Informed consent for accepting medical treatment
C) Written consent by a rape victim
D) Request for euthanasia by the patient
E) Participation in a research trial authorized by an Institutional Review Board
Correct Answer: D) Request for euthanasia by the patient
Explanation: Euthanasia is illegal in Jordan and is not a type of consent. The other options are recognized forms: implied, informed, written (expressed), and research consent approved by an IRB.
###
Which of the following is NOT related to the World Medical Association declarations concerning the humanization of health services?
A) Declaration of Helsinki
B) United Nations Development Fund for Women
C) Declaration of Geneva
D) Declaration of Tokyo
E) Declaration of Oslo
Correct Answer: B) United Nations Development Fund for Women
Explanation: Helsinki, Geneva, Tokyo, and Oslo are all World Medical Association declarations. The United Nations Development Fund for Women is a United Nations agency and not a medical declaration.
###
Breach of patient confidentiality is permissible in which of the following cases?
A) Request by the patient's husband or wife
B) Request by an insurance company
C) Reporting rheumatoid arthritis to the patient's employer
D) Request by a relative of the patient
E) Upon the request or consent of the patient
Correct Answer: E) Upon the request or consent of the patient
Explanation: Confidentiality may be breached with the patient's own consent, for notifiable diseases, by court order, for child protection, or when there is a serious risk to others. Requests from a spouse, insurer, employer, or relative do not justify a breach.
