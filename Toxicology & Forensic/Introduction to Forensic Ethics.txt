When dealing with vulnerable children (victims of violence), what is the MAIN priority of the physician from an ethical point of view?
A) Respect confidentiality
B) Protection through early detection
C) Protection of secrecy
D) Respect autonomy of parents
E) Notification to the authority
Correct Answer: B) Protection through early detection
Explanation: The physician's primary ethical duty with child abuse victims is early identification and protection, which takes priority over confidentiality or parental autonomy.
###
Breach of confidentiality is permissible in which case?
A) Upon request of the patient
B) Wife/husband request
C) Insurance company request
D) Reporting rheumatoid arthritis to employer
Correct Answer: A) Upon request of the patient
Explanation: Confidentiality may be breached with the patient's own consent, for notifiable diseases, court order, or public safety — spousal, insurer, or employer requests without patient consent do not justify it.
###
Breaching confidentiality is permissible in:
A) Wife/husband request
B) Insurance representative request
C) Report cholera case (notifiable disease)
Correct Answer: C) Report cholera case (notifiable disease)
Explanation: Notifiable/communicable diseases like cholera must be reported to public health authorities by law regardless of patient consent, due to public health risk.
###
All but one of the following is related to the bioethics principles (code of ethics):
A) Justice
B) Reluctant
C) Beneficence
D) Non-maleficence
Correct Answer: B) Reluctant
Explanation: The four bioethics pillars are autonomy, beneficence, non-maleficence, and justice; "reluctant" is not a recognized bioethical principle.
###
One of the following is NOT correct concerning valid consent prior to medical treatment:
A) Voluntary and freely given
B) Consent of partner (Husband/Wife) is always mandatory
C) Informed of what the treatment involves
D) Informed including benefits and risks
E) Examining capacity of patient
Correct Answer: B) Consent of partner (Husband/Wife) is always mandatory
Explanation: A competent adult patient can independently give valid consent; requiring spousal consent would violate patient autonomy.
###
Regarding consent — a 23-year-old single adult female with vaginal bleeding. Which statement is correct?
A) Medical consent must be obtained from her father or husband
B) Consent of partner is always mandatory
C) A guardian must be present
D) An adult patient can give her own medical consent independently
Correct Answer: D) An adult patient can give her own medical consent independently
Explanation: Any competent adult has full legal capacity to consent to medical treatment without parental or spousal authorization.
###
The utmost important rule when doing a physical examination of intimate parts of the body is:
A) A chaperon must be present
B) Written consent is mandatory
C) Only a same-sex physician may examine
D) A family member must be present
Correct Answer: A) A chaperon must be present
Explanation: A chaperon safeguards both the patient's dignity and the physician's professional integrity during intimate examinations.
###
"Respect for Persons" in the ethical approach might mean any of the following EXCEPT:
A) Autonomy
B) Integrity
C) Follow doctor's order
D) Equity
E) Equality
Correct Answer: C) Follow doctor's order
Explanation: Respect for persons centers on autonomy, integrity, equity, and equality, which empower the patient — blind obedience to the doctor is the opposite of patient-centred ethics.
###
One of the following is NOT TRUE concerning duties of doctors to patients:
A) Respect a patient's right to confidentiality
B) Always bear in mind the obligation to respect human life
C) Informed consent is not necessary when dealing with victims of violence
D) Whenever examination is beyond the physician's capacity, refer to another physician
E) Act in the patient's best interest when providing medical care
Correct Answer: C) Informed consent is not necessary when dealing with victims of violence
Explanation: Informed consent is always required except in true emergencies where delay would cause harm; being a violence victim does not remove this requirement.
###
One of the following characters is NOT appropriate considering ethical standards:
A) Maintain professional competence throughout career
B) Enhance intimate relationships with patients
C) Respect patient confidentiality
D) Enhance multidisciplinary approach
E) Deal honestly with patients
Correct Answer: B) Enhance intimate relationships with patients
Explanation: Romantic or intimate relationships between physicians and patients violate professional boundaries and are strictly unethical.
###
One of the following is odd and not known to be relevant to world medical declarations related to humanization of health service:
A) Declaration of Helsinki
B) United Nations development fund for women
C) Declaration of Geneva
D) Medical trials on animals
E) United Nations children fund
Correct Answer: B) United Nations development fund for women
Explanation: Recognized WHO/WMA medical declarations include Helsinki and Geneva; the UN development fund for women is not a medical declaration.
###
One of the following is odd and not well known to be relevant to world medical declarations related to humanization of health service:
A) Declaration of Geneva
B) Declaration of London
C) Declaration of Oslo
D) Declaration of Tokyo
E) Declaration of Helsinki
Correct Answer: B) Declaration of London
Explanation: WMA declarations include Geneva, Helsinki, Oslo, and Tokyo; there is no formally recognized "Declaration of London" in medical ethics.
###
Humanization of medical practice was the main theme in:
A) Nuremberg code
B) Geneva declaration code
C) Oslo declaration code
D) Tokyo declaration code
E) Ottawa declaration code
Correct Answer: B) Geneva declaration code
Explanation: The Declaration of Geneva (1948) was the WMA's post-WWII commitment to humanize medical practice, forming the modern physician's oath.
###
Declaration of Helsinki is related to:
A) Violence
B) Child abuse
C) Humans in medical research
D) Torture of prisoners
Correct Answer: C) Humans in medical research
Explanation: The Declaration of Helsinki (1964) establishes ethical standards specifically for medical research involving human subjects.
###
Types of consent — all EXCEPT one:
A) Requesting euthanasia by patient
B) Accepting medical treatment
C) Implied consent for medical examination
D) Written consent by rape victim
E) Involvement in a research trial authorized by IRB
Correct Answer: A) Requesting euthanasia by patient
Explanation: Euthanasia is not a valid consent scenario for treatment — it is illegal in most jurisdictions, unlike the other listed valid consent types.
