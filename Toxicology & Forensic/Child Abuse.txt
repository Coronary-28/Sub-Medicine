Which of the following is NOT a cause or indicator of child abuse?
A) Multiple bruises
B) Shaken baby syndrome
C) Multiple sclerosis
D) Munchausen syndrome by proxy
Correct Answer: C) Multiple sclerosis
Explanation: MS is a neurological autoimmune disease unrelated to abuse; multiple bruises, shaken baby syndrome, and Munchausen by proxy are classic abuse indicators.
###
Bruising in which of the following areas is NOT considered a suspicious sign of child abuse?
A) Knee
B) Chest
C) Abdomen
D) Back
Correct Answer: A) Knee
Explanation: Knee bruising is a common, normal childhood injury from falls and play; abuse-related bruising is characteristically found on protected areas such as the torso, back, abdomen, and chest.
###
Regarding Munchausen syndrome by proxy, all of the following are true EXCEPT:
A) Illness does not suggest a particular disease
B) The child becomes ill in the presence of the caregiver
C) Laboratory and radiographic findings are negative
D) Parents readily admit to causing the illness
Correct Answer: D) Parents readily admit to causing the illness
Explanation: Munchausen by proxy is characterized by unexplained illness that improves when the child is separated from the caregiver, a lack of clear diagnosis, and negative workup; perpetrators rarely confess to causing the illness.
