Which of the following is NOT a limitation of pulse oximetry?
A) Severe anemia (Hb 7–10 g/dL)
B) Carbon monoxide poisoning
C) Severe vasoconstriction
D) Hypertension
E) Nail polish or artificial nails
Correct Answer: D) Hypertension
Explanation: Hypertension does not interfere with the optical signal. Inaccuracy arises from poor pulsatile flow (vasoconstriction, shock, hypothermia), dyshemoglobinemias (carboxyhemoglobin reads falsely normal), anemia, and optical interference.

How is acute laryngospasm managed?
A) IM epinephrine as first-line for all cases
B) Remove the trigger, call for help, perform a jaw thrust, and give 100% oxygen with continuous positive-pressure ventilation
C) Immediate surgical cricothyrotomy
D) Atropine
E) Nebulized corticosteroids
Correct Answer: B) Remove the trigger, call for help, perform a jaw thrust, and give 100% oxygen with continuous positive-pressure ventilation
Explanation: Refractory complete laryngospasm may require deepening anesthesia and a neuromuscular blocker such as succinylcholine. IM epinephrine is first-line only when the presentation is anaphylaxis.

After help is called for an unresponsive, pulseless adult, what should be done while waiting?
A) Wait for EMS to arrive
B) Recheck the pulse repeatedly
C) Intubate the patient
D) Establish IV access
E) Begin high-quality CPR immediately
Correct Answer: E) Begin high-quality CPR immediately
Explanation: Immediate high-quality CPR maintains coronary and cerebral perfusion until defibrillation or advanced care is available.

Which device analyzes the cardiac rhythm during basic life support and advises whether a shock is needed?
A) 12-lead ECG machine
B) Transcutaneous pacemaker
C) Automated external defibrillator (AED)
D) Pulse oximeter
E) Capnograph
Correct Answer: C) Automated external defibrillator (AED)
Explanation: The AED analyzes the rhythm and advises a shock when indicated. The chest is exposed and dried, pads are attached, the patient is cleared during analysis and shock, and CPR is resumed immediately afterward.

Which lateral neck radiographic sign is classically associated with epiglottitis?
A) Thumbprint sign
B) Steeple sign
C) Widened prevertebral soft-tissue space
D) Subglottic narrowing
E) Radiopaque foreign body
Correct Answer: A) Thumbprint sign
Explanation: The thumbprint sign reflects a swollen epiglottis. In a toxic child with suspected epiglottitis, imaging must never delay expert airway management.

All of the following are considered minimal precautions for preventing the spread of communicable disease during resuscitation, EXCEPT:
A) Water-impervious leggings
B) Face mask
C) Goggles
D) Needle-impermeable sterile gloves
E) Water-impervious gown
Correct Answer: D) Needle-impermeable sterile gloves
Explanation: Minimal barrier precautions consist of a water-impervious gown and leggings, a face mask, and eye protection. Needle-impermeable sterile gloves are not part of the minimal set.
